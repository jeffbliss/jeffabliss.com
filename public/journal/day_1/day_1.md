9:20 PM I don't even know where to begin guys. Today has been such an incredible day. I am lying in my tent right now on top of my sleeping bag because it is so warm outside. I actually ran out of water on the approach trail up and was mildly dehydrated!! I have met so many wonderful and amazing people today that I don't think I could document them all. Keri, Donna, Lil Cub, Adam, Josh, Bob, Alejandra, and so many more. I know I am forgetting some. Each one of them has such an incredible story as to why they are out here. I love listening to theirs and sharing mine in return. However, I did experience the sadder side of the trail today. I knew coming into this that 70% of potential thru-hikers never make it to Maine and unfortunately I witnessed someone turn around today. I saw her sitting on a log by herself crying and she just got off the phone. I asked her if everything was alright, asked for her name, and she told me that she wasn't as strong as she thought she was. She called her dad and he was coming to pick her up at the falls. I tried my best to show her that she is indeed strong and encourage her to come to Springer with me but she turned around. Broke my heart. Hope she gets some much needed **TLC** and makes it back out here. There are so many people up here at Springer mountain. There has to be at least 20 people camping around here and most of them are thru-hikers. I cannot wait to get to know each of them better and share this experience. I have reception up here but there's no way I could possibly upload all of the photos on this crappy signal. Everyone will have to wait till **Neels Gap** most likely to see these posts! One experience I did learn tonight is that I won't tell people exact times I'll meet them on the trail haha. I told my parents that we would meet them on Springer at 5 and I found out quickly that I would be wrong. I had to go ahead of Matt and Chris and also hike at a pretty nice pace to get up there by 530. It was still so worth it though to meet my mom and dad up here. Thank you two again for everything. I leave off tonight with photos from today. It has been a wonderful first day everyone. A lot buggier and hotter than I anticipated though. I'm actually getting bit as I type this ha. **Appalachian Trail** day one out!
